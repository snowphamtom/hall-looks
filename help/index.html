A friendly field guide

Help

Start with a Look, build on the Forge, and keep your favorites in the Locker. Every control on this site does what its label says, right away.

How do I say a character?

Open Create and tap short names: ancestry (Elf is there), origin (Dark wood is there), then build, weapon, and a rank. A figure is drawn from your picks, and the page writes a markdown sheet. Say the line at the top, like elf, dark wood.

How do I design a character?

  1. Open Forge.
  2. Pick the left or right figure, then one of the four looks, or keep the blank figure.
  3. Change helmet, chest, finish, color, weapon and the eight sliders.
  4. Open Locker at the top and press Save to locker.

What are Looks?

Looks are ready-made fighters: Foundation, Black Chrome, White Samurai, Skull Champion, and Titan Gladiator. On the four fighter pages, each style button shows a separate picture of that fighter, made ahead of time. The prompt box there writes words only; it does not draw.

Forge sliders

  • Wear — how worn the armor looks.
  • Cracks — how many cracks show in the armor.
  • Armor glow — how bright the armor lights are (drawn on the blank figure).
  • Weapon length — how long the weapon is.
  • Battle damage — dents, burns and chips on the weapon.
  • Weapon glow — how bright the weapon glows.
  • Muscle — how heavy the build is.
  • Height — how tall the figure stands.

The three weapon sliders and Armor glow draw on the blank figure. On one of the four looks they change the spec only.

Forge buttons

  • Copy spec — copies the settings as text.
  • Save spec file — downloads the settings as a file.
  • Save picture — downloads a picture of the canvas.
  • Save to locker — keeps the character in your locker on this browser.
  • Load — puts a saved character on the selected figure.
  • Remove — deletes a saved character, after you confirm.
  • Keep today's crown — keeps today's crown for good. Then it reads Crown kept until tomorrow.

Load and Remove only show next to a saved character.

What is Locker?

Locker is one locker for the whole site, kept in this browser on this device. It holds 24. Saves from Forge, Create, Foundation and the Locker roster all land there. The page background follows the highest saved rank, and only Prism (rank 24) is holographic.

Smith and Colosseum

Smith shows the weapon picture that goes with each look; your note shows beside it as words. Colosseum shows one fight picture for each pairing of two different looks, made ahead of time. No fight is simulated.

House rules

  • There is no currency anywhere.
  • Level shows only as armor wear.
  • One crown war a day.
  • The hall of champions is not a live ranking. It updates only when someone has a crown and a full locker of 24.
  • The ultimate champion gets a custom crown and an iridescent, holographic locker character.
  • Ranking is by originality, difficulty, creativeness, realistic application and rarity.
  • Trades are barter only and need both sides to agree. They cover saved characters, weapons, armor, designs and techniques.
  • Outside sellers get only the pay-to-play practice zone, with practice swords and commoner clothing.

These are the rules of the game. This site does not run crown wars, trades, rankings or a practice zone yet; it has no accounts and nothing leaves your browser.

Common questions

  • Can I start over? Yes. Clear the Locker to remove its saved characters. Crowns stay.
  • Why did the background change? Blossom drifts behind everything; the page takes on the color wash of your single highest saved rank.
  • Why is only one rank shiny? Prism is the one holographic rank by design.
  • Will my locker follow me? No. It lives in this browser only.
