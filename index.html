HEAVY IS THE CROWN

The weight sits.

A dark hall of fighters. Open a look, build on the Forge, or keep what you save in the locker. The site background follows the highest rank you have saved; only Prism is holographic.

  1. Room — this screen: the hall, then the looks below.
  2. Looks — Foundation, Black Chrome, White Samurai, Skull Champion, Titan Gladiator. On the four fighter pages, pick a style to see a separate picture of that fighter.
  3. Create — tap a type you can say, like elf, dark wood. A figure is drawn from your picks, and the sheet is markdown.
  4. Forge — two figures on a canvas. Start from one of the four looks or the blank figure, change armor, weapon and body with eight sliders, save to the locker, or save a picture.
  5. Locker — one locker for the whole site, kept in this browser. It holds 24. Prism is rank 24.
