The Two Sinks

INTO 31 → 162 · INTO 63 → 141

Baseline is 148.75 doors per node. Kirchhoff holds at all 48: hallway count equals sink count, everywhere.

EXHIBIT 02 · SEVENTEEN GEMS

Derived from the system, then verified. Each one survived its own check.

  1. 01 — H₁₄ is constant-weight: every column weighs exactly 8 of 14.
  2. 02 — Its difference set is complete: the 1,128 pair XORs take exactly 63 values — every nonzero 7-bit vector with bit 0 equal to 0.
  3. 03 — The 544 identity: the distance-6 doors are exactly the complementary pairs — 17 pairs × 32 = 544, all Floor.
  4. 04 — The door involution (a,b,c,d) ↔ (a,b,d,c) has no fixed points, so every pair’s door count is even.
  5. 05 — Sinks verified: into 31 → 162, into 63 → 141; masks 27, 23, 15 tie at 155.
  6. 06 — Reversibility folds the doors into exactly 1,785 quads.
  7. 07 — Partner pairs are balanced — identical door counts, zero mismatches; 944 door pairs form 280 partnership components.
  8. 08 — Clean doors are forced: c = a & b for all 360.
  9. 09 — No mask is exiled: all 48 serve as both sink and hallway.
  10. 10 — The H₁₄ difference set is the diagonal {(x, x)}; its distance distribution is H₇’s, doubled.
  11. 11 — Kirchhoff for doors: hallway equals sink at all 48 nodes; pair-membership equals hall + sink (node 31: 324 = 162 + 162).
  12. 12 — W is invertible, barely: smallest |λ| ≈ 0.059, determinant negative.
  13. 13 — W degeneracy: 27 distinct eigenvalues — 15 simple, 3 double, 9 triple.
  14. 14 — T’s total appearances: 28,560 = 4 × 7,140.
  15. 15 — A refused candidate: reflection through the window’s center preserves only 3,780 of 7,140 doors. Not a symmetry; recorded as refused.
  16. 16 — A rhyme, refused: 14,280 = 14,280 by two unrelated mechanisms. No identity claimed.
  17. 17 — The tollbooth split: low-bit popcount-2 masks carry 134; high-bit carry 155. Position, not weight, sets the toll.

EXHIBIT 03 · THE PLATES, GRADED

Ten plates rendering this system were produced independently and later checked, claim by claim, against recomputation. 54 of 56 checkable claims are exact — 96.4%. The exhibit prints its corrections:

Two claims are false as printed, both on one plate: “3,384 Wider” — the verified count is 3,584, and the plate’s own bins sum to 6,940, not 7,140 — and “46 Odd Masks” — there are 48.

Two more plates are portraits with caveats: the heatmap’s pixels decode to operator T, not the W its title claims (a swapped portrait); the spectrum bars decode to neither operator and stand as schematic.

EXHIBIT 04 · THE REFUSALS

What the system would not admit, and what we would not claim. A ledger that hides its refusals is not a ledger.

The Refused Reflection

Reflection through the window’s center (m → 100 − m) preserves only 52.9% of the doors. It looks like a symmetry and is not one. Recorded as refused — Gem 15.

The Refused Rhyme

14,280 = 14,280

Twice the same number, by two unrelated mechanisms — H₁₄’s signed-triple alias count, and a transition total. A rhyme is not a reason. No identity claimed — Gem 16.

The True Thing’s Edge

H₁₄ IS PAIR-PERFECT — NOT TRIPLE-PERFECT

Its decoder repairs every signed single and pair uniquely, then aliases on 14,280 signed triples, each one completing a door. The stronger v4 construction posts 0 at that gate. Even verified things have edges; the exhibit marks this one.

The Mislabeled Portraits

The heatmap wears W’s title over T’s pixels; the spectrum bars decode to no operator at all. Both hang in Exhibit 03 with their corrections printed beside them — refused as evidence for what they claim, kept as evidence of the grading.
